Thekedaar   is a two - sided digital marketplace that connects customers with local, verified  home service professionals and construction contractors   —   all in one app. It has two sections:  Chhota Kaam (micro services) and Bada Kaam (macro projects). Unlike Urban Compan y,  Thekedaar does not train professionals   —   it digitizes the skilled local experts who already  exist in every Indian neighbourhood.  1. The Problem We Are Solving  India has a massive, fragmented home services market. Every household needs plumbers, electricians,  cleaners, painters, AC mechanics, and contractors   —   but finding trustworthy, skilled professionals is a  pain point that has not been solved for Tier 2 and T ier 3 cities.  From the Customer's Side:  •   No reliable way to find skilled local professionals digitally.  •   Trust is built only through word - of - mouth   —   'jaanpehchaan' referrals.  •   No price transparency   —   haggling wastes time and creates frustration.  •   No accountability if work quality is poor   —   especially in big projects.  •   Platforms like Urban Company are English - first, Tier 1 city focused.  From the Professional's Side:  •   Skilled local professionals (AC mechanic, plumber, painter) are limited to 10 - 15 known  customers.  •   No digital identity   —   invisible to the broader market.  •   Income is seasonal and unpredictable.

Trust & Safety Framework  Trust is the single most important factor in this market   —   especially in Tier 2/3 cities where  'jaanpehchaan' (word - of - mouth) drives all decisions. Thekedaar digitizes this trust through multiple  layers:  5.1 Professional Verification  •   Aadhaar + PAN verification   —   mandatory for all professionals.  •   Video introduction uploaded to profile   —   customer sees the face before booking.  •   Skill category self - declaration   —   verified by initial customer reviews.  •   Background check for macro project contractors.  5.2 Community Trust System  •   Hyperlocal reviews   —   reviews from customers in the same locality carry higher weight.  •   'Aapke mohalle mein X kaam kiye hain'   —   platform shows locality - specific job count.  •   Neighbour verification badge   —   if someone from your neighbourhood has used and rated this  professional.  •   Inspired by: Nextdoor (neighbourhood trust), Airbnb ('your friend stayed here').  5.3 Payment Protection   —   Milestone Model  Payment Stage   Micro Services   Macro Projects  Advance   Full payment via escrow at booking   30% released on project start  Mid - point   N/A   40% released on mid - point  inspection  Completion   Released after customer  confirmation  30% released after final approval  Dispute   Refund / re - service within 48 hours   Security deposit + mediation cell  5.4 Dispute Resolution  •   Contractor Security Deposit: All contractors deposit a refundable amount at onboarding. Proven  disputes are compensated from this deposit.  •   Rectification Window: Professional gets 7 days to fix issues before penalty is applied.  •   Platform Mediation Cell: Dedicated team reviews photo/video evidence and gives verdict.  •   Insurance Tie - up (Phase 2): Partner with Bajaj Allianz / ICICI Lombard for project insurance on  large jobs.
